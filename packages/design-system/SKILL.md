Quick rules: Quicksand type, indigo primary #1700a2, .25rem radius (cards .5rem), cards have borders not shadows, Eva Icons outline + Dadosfera custom module icons, Brazilian Portuguese sentence-case copy, tabs uppercase, buttons never uppercase.
